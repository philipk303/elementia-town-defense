TITLE SCREEN — Elementia Town Defense
Target use: client/index.html lobby background. Landscape, 1920x1080 (16:9).
Style: realistic 3D-rendered anime (think a AAA game key-art render, not chibi/flat-shaded) —
volumetric lighting, real material shaders (metal, cloth, stone, embers), painterly anime facial
proportions but full adult body proportions, NOT the game's small chibi in-game sprites.

SCENE:
A wide, dusk-lit feudal-Japanese hilltop village under siege, seen from a slightly low
heroic angle. In the background: a wooden palisade wall, a stone watchtower (yagura) with a
curved tile roof, and a torii gate silhouetted against a deep orange-to-indigo sunset sky with
drifting embers and mist. Rice-paddy terraces and lantern-lit market stalls sit in the middle
distance.

FOUR HERO CHARACTERS in the foreground, each visibly using their elemental power, arranged in a
dynamic action line-up (not a static lineup — overlapping poses, weight on different legs, as if
about to clash with an unseen threat):

1. FIRE HERO — young adult male, saturated orange hair styled in a topknot, wearing lacquered
   orange samurai-style armor with glowing ember/crack detailing. Wielding a long curved saber
   that is wreathed in real flame, mid-swing. Skin lightly lit by firelight from below.

2. WATER HERO — young adult, blue hair, flowing dark-blue and teal robes with droplet/wave
   accent patterns. Conjuring a curling wave or orb of water around one hand, water droplets
   suspended mid-air, cool blue rim-light.

3. EARTH HERO — young adult, brown hair, sturdy grounded stance, brown/stone-toned armor with
   visible stone-fleck/mineral texture. Summoning jagged rock spikes or a floating boulder from
   the ground, hands planted low, warm earthy lighting.

4. WIND HERO — young adult female, pale-cyan flowing hair, pale cyan/off-white robe with a dark
   outline/trim (important: keep her silhouette readable against a bright sky — do not render her
   in pure white). Holding a pair of compact fans, a visible swirl of wind/gust lines and flower
   petals or leaves caught in the current around her.

All four should read as a cohesive matched set: same semi-realistic 3D-anime rendering style,
same lighting environment (dusk, embers, mist), same scale, standing together as a team on a
stone courtyard in front of the palisade/watchtower/torii.

TITLE TEXT: "ELEMENTIA TOWN DEFENSE" — large, bold, brush-stroke/calligraphy-influenced display
lettering (Japanese-brush aesthetic, still legible Latin characters), placed in the upper third
or lower third of the frame with enough negative space/dark vignette behind it to stay readable
over the busy scene. Suggested accent colors for the title: aged gold (#c9a227) or shu-red
(#b23a2f) with a dark outline/shadow.

Do NOT include any watermark, extra logos, or UI chrome — this is a pure background/key-art
render, all game UI will be composited on top of it separately.
